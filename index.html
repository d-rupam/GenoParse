GenoParse: The Sequence Compiler

[ THE METAPHOR ] In software engineering, "parsing" is the command to convert raw, unstructured strings into functional arrays. GenoParse directly fuses this computer science architecture with nucleotide biology.

This utility operates strictly as a client-side engine. It ingests unsequenced FASTA files and compiles DNA, RNA, and Protein translations securely within your local browser memory.

Absolute Data Sovereignty applies here: the raw genetic payload never leaves your machine, and the molecular translation is instantaneous.
