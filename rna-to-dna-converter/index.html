About the Developer

GenoParse was conceptualized and developed by Rupam Das, an undergraduate Microbiology researcher blending wet-lab biology with client-side web infrastructure. This tool was engineered to solve the latency and data-privacy issues found in traditional server-based bioinformatics utilities.

How to Execute Reverse Transcription

  • Input the RNA: Paste your RNA sequence or full FASTA record into the left panel. Alternatively, drag and drop a `.fasta` file.
  • Sanitization: The engine will automatically strip headers and spaces, isolating only valid RNA bases.
  • Execute: Click "Reverse Transcribe". The logic instantly converts all Uracil (U) bases back to Thymine (T), rebuilding the DNA coding strand.
  • Export: Use the "Copy Text" button to copy the raw DNA, or click "Download FASTA" to export the file.
